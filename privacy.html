Privacy Notice

This notice describes the types of personal information NovaBank may handle and how that information may be used and shared.

Information we may collect

Depending on the services you use, information may include details you provide, such as your name, contact information, account choices, and messages sent to us. Information may also be created when you use banking services, such as account and transaction records.

How information may be used

Information may be used to provide and maintain services, respond to questions, protect accounts, prevent fraud, improve services, and meet legal and regulatory obligations.

When information may be shared

Information may be shared with service providers that support NovaBank’s operations, when needed to complete or service a transaction, or when disclosure is permitted or required by law. Applicable choices and limits depend on the information and service involved.

Protecting and keeping information

Appropriate safeguards should be used to protect personal information. Information should be kept only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law.

Your choices and questions

Privacy choices and rights may depend on the services you use and where you live. For questions about this notice, please contact NovaBank.

Updates to this notice

This notice may be updated as NovaBank’s services and information practices change. Any updated notice will be made available here.
